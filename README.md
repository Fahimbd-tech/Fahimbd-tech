# 👋 About Me

I am **MD ABDUR RAHMAN FAHIM**, a **Graphic Designer & Creative Contributor** based in Sylhet, Bangladesh.

I work on logos, posters, banners, business cards, thumbnails and other digital visual design, shaped by each client's needs and brand identity.

My interest in design began around **2016–17**, with regular client and business work since **2019**.

My design practice covers:

- Brand Identity
- Logo Design
- Posters
- Campaign Graphics
- Banners
- Social Media Design
- Business Cards
- Digital Visual Design
- Bengali Typography
- UI Design
- Information Design
- Video Editing
- Motion

I work with **ABO Enterprise** as a Graphic Designer & Creative Contributor.

I have also worked on graphic design for the **Bairagibazar Cricket League (BBCL)** and **Chatol Sports**, including graphic design responsibilities for their Facebook pages, and on most of the graphic design of the official **Jago Media** page, according to the portfolio statement.
